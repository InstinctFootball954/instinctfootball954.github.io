Prepare

Instincts only get better when the preparation is right. Footwork, film, and a plan for every rep, before you ever line up.

Anticipate

Read the alignment before the snap and the movement after it. Know where the defense should be before it gets there.

Act

When you already know what's coming, you play fast. Clean, efficient movement with no wasted steps and no second-guessing.

Coach Travis

The name Instinct means something to me. You never stop improving your instincts, and the only way to improve them is to prepare the right way.

As a receiver, I learned to read where the defense should be while running my route, from the alignment before the snap to the movement after it. That is what let me flow through routes instead of guessing. I played the game at a high level, and now I teach athletes how to see it and apply it to their own game.

  • Former college wide receiver
  • Current high school assistant football coach
  • Trains WR, QB, RB, DB, LB, and kickers

Questions

What should my athlete bring?

Cleats, athletic clothes, and a full water bottle. I bring the balls, cones, and all other equipment.

Can parents watch?

Yes. Parents are welcome at every session.

What about the heat?

Sessions are scheduled early morning and evening when possible, with water breaks throughout.

Do you train athletes from any school?

Yes. Training is open to athletes from any school or program.

How do I send film for review?

After you join, send a link to your clips from Hudl, YouTube, or Google Drive. Include the play times you want looked at and your position.
